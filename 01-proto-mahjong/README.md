## Cross-comparison

**Evidence** · `corroborated` — two or more independent sources agree on the facts that define this variant

What each source actually says, as fetched and read:

- **[2](../REFERENCES.md#ref-2)** — Sloperama's family tree page gives the Matiao / Khanhoo / dominoes root structure the chart's root node stands for
- **[26](../REFERENCES.md#ref-26)** — Stanwick and Xu's tile-set research on early ma que, underlying the Hua Maque and Wang Maque material
- **[26](../REFERENCES.md#ref-26)** — dates the physical evidence: the ma que pai sets of Carl Himly and George Glover were collected in 1868-1876, 1872-1873 and 1875, and the wind tiles are found in most known ma que pai sets after the 1870s. The game is 麻雀 ma que, literally 'hemp bird' and in bonded form 'sparrow', also known in the Late Qing period as peng hu, 'encountering harmony'

**Reading it:** The chart's 'c. 1870s' is corroborated, though by artefacts rather than by rules. Stanwick and Xu date surviving ma que pai sets to collections made between 1868 and 1876, and note that wind tiles appear in most known sets after the 1870s. That supports the decade without touching the chart's other claim, that the rules are lost, which no source contradicts either. The node is therefore well dated and genuinely ruleless.
