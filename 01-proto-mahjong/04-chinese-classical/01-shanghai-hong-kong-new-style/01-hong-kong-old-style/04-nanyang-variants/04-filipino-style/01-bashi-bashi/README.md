**Reading it:** Sloperama documents two incompatible games under this name: version A with 144 tiles and 8 red fives, version B with 112 tiles and 6 red fives, differing also on scoring and payout. The chart carries one undifferentiated node. Sloperama also says Bashi-Bashi is based on Japanese modern AND Filipino rules, while the chart descends it from Filipino alone.
