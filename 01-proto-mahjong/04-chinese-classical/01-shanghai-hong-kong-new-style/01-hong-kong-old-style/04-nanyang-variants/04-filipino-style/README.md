- **[1](../../../../../../REFERENCES.md#ref-1)** — kongs get paid at the instant they are melded
- **[58](../../../../../../REFERENCES.md#ref-58)** — cited by Sloperama's own catalogue. Confirms both the four plant flowers and the four season tiles, and describes a separate flower wall used for replacements
- **[54](../../../../../../REFERENCES.md#ref-54)** — states the game uses no jokers, which contradicts Sloperama's note that certain tiles may be designated as jokers under a rule it could not describe

**Reading it:** Three sources agree on the 144-tile set and the 16-tile hand winning on 17. More importantly, Wikipedia independently confirms the flower handling that the chart's own new caveat makes the test for telling Filipino from Taiwanese: dragons and winds are grouped with the flowers. The one disagreement is jokers, which Sloperama hedges and Wikipedia denies; prefer Wikipedia, since Sloperama admits it cannot state the rule.

All sources above were fetched and read on 2026-09-18. Nothing here is second-hand: a claim that could not be read at its source is not in this table.
