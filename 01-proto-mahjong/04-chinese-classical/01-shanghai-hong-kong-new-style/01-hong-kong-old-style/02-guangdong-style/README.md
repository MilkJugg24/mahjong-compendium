- **[42](../../../../../REFERENCES.md#ref-42)** — Guangdong Style (廣東十三張麻將) is described as popular in South China AND TAIWAN, based on HKOS but using a large number of 'New Style' winning conditions and an additive scoring system. Its timeline reads: 1970s, New Style became associated with gambling in HK and a 'back to basics' variant called Clear Chapter or HKOS was adopted, winning conditions shrunk and fu removed; 1980s, New Style conditions were added back and fan were removed in favour of an additive system, and Guangdong Style was born
- **[46](../../../../../REFERENCES.md#ref-46)** — although Guangdong is a mainland province, Guangdong New Style has more in common with variants coming out of Hong Kong, and Taiwan post-1990s; it never uses Joys or Treasure Tiles, almost always allows multiple winners on a discard, and almost always uses discarder-pays-all

**Reading it:** Two wiki pages agree that Guangdong Style is a 13-tile game built on HKOS with New Style winning conditions and additive scoring, born in the 1980s, matching the chart. Both also independently support the chart's caveat about influence running two ways: one calls it popular in South China and Taiwan, the other says it has more in common with Hong Kong, and with Taiwan post-1990s, than with its mainland neighbours.

All sources above were fetched and read on 2026-09-18. Nothing here is second-hand: a claim that could not be read at its source is not in this table.
