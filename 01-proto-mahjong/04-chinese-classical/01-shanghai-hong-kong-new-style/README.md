- **[1](../../../REFERENCES.md#ref-1)** — Sloperama's 'Hong Kong New Style': many many special hands, vs few in Old Style
- **[28](../../../REFERENCES.md#ref-28)** — wiki treats New Chapter as the transition from HKOS toward Guangdong style
- **[43](../../../REFERENCES.md#ref-43)** — CORROBORATES THE INTERMEDIATE NODE: states that HKOS was adopted as a backlash to HKNS, which had itself evolved from Chinese Classical, and that the simplified variant born from that backlash is Qing Zhang, Clear Chapter, known in English as Hong Kong Old Style. That places a new-style stage between Chinese Classical and HKOS, which is exactly what this node is

**Reading it:** The node's existence is now independently supported rather than inherited. Sloperama's Hong Kong New Style entry matches on tiles, hand and payout, and its 'many many special hands' against Old Style's 'few' is the substantive difference the branch depends on. The wiki's mainland page describes later pattern additions to the classical game as Shanghai New Style, and its GMCR page states outright that HKOS was adopted as a backlash to HKNS, which had evolved from Chinese Classical. Three sources therefore place a new-style stage between Chinese Classical and Hong Kong Old Style. What none of them does is date it to the 1920s.

All sources above were fetched and read on 2026-09-18. Nothing here is second-hand: a claim that could not be read at its source is not in this table.
