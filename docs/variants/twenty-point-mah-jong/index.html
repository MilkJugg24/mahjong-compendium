Reading it

1924, R.F. Foster, and the 20-point minimum that gives the game its name all confirm the chart. Sloperama adds the motive and the verdict: Foster's attempt satisfied nobody but paved the way for American mah-jongg in the 1930s, which supports the chart's placement of it beside the American line rather than in it.
