Reading it

Sloperama and the wiki agree on 136 tiles, a 13-tile hand and point-and-double scoring, and the Krakow Chombo Club history independently dates the first Japanese mahjong organisation to 1929. Sloperama's early-forms table adds real structural evidence: Japanese Classical scores identically with the 1920s New Method and only 4 against standard Chinese Classical.
