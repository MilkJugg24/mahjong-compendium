Reading it

Sloperama and the wiki agree on 144 tiles, a 13-tile hand and pattern scoring; the wiki adds the 8-point minimum. Wikipedia independently dates the sport certification to January 1998 and the rules to that September, matching the chart. One wrinkle: the wiki's infobox is built on a 2006 rulebook, not the 1998 edition the chart cites.
