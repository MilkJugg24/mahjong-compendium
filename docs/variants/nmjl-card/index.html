corroborated — two or more independent sources agree on the facts that define this variant

  • [1] in 1937 four women, Viola Cecil, Dorothy Meyerson, Herma Jacobs and Hortense Potter, founded the National Mah Jongg League in New York City; nearly 400 women rallied at Manhattan's Essex House hotel to support standardising American rules
  • [2] dates the founding to 1937 in New York City, by a group of predominantly Jewish women from the Upper West Side
  • [3] dates the founding to 1937 in New York City
  • [4] dates the NMJL's start to 1937
  • [5] CONFLICT: states that the National Mah Jongg League, Inc. published a volume of 'Official American Rules' in 1935, which predates the 1937 founding date the other four sources give
  • [6] RESOLVES THE CONFLICT: the League's own site, selling the 2026 card, describes itself as celebrating 89 years. Counting back from 2026 gives 1937, which is the League's own dating of its founding

Reading it

The conflict is settled. Four secondary sources date the founding to 1937 in New York City, two of them naming the Essex House meeting, and the League's own website now confirms it from the inside: in 2026 it celebrates 89 years, which counts back to 1937. Wikipedia's statement that the League published Official American Rules in 1935 cannot be reconciled with that and should be treated as the outlier; whatever the 1935 publication was, the organisation dates itself from 1937.
