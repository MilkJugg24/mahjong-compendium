Reading it

Sloperama's early-forms table puts numbers on this node. The 1903 Li column scores YES on all four Chinese Classical hallmarks and NO on the three new-style ones, and rates 6 similarity to Chinese Classical against only 2 to HKOS. That is independent structural support for placing Li 1903 on the ancestral side of the tree.
